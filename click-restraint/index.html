No, it is generally not recommended to microwave plastic.

While containers labeled "microwave safe" are tested by the FDA to ensure they will not melt or warp from the heat, health experts increasingly warn that heating plastics can still release microplastics, nanoplastics, and endocrine-disrupting chemicals directly into your food.BPBeyond Plastics +3

The Hidden Risks of Heating Plastic

  • Chemical Leaching: Heat causes chemical additives like Bisphenol A (BPA) and phthalates to migrate out of the plastic and contaminate your food. These act as endocrine disruptors that mimic or block natural hormones.

AI Overview

No, your phone is not passively recording your everyday conversations to serve you ads. Listening to and transcribing billions of people's conversations would be technically impossible and drain your battery. Instead, companies use sophisticated algorithms to predict what you will talk about next.◉CBS News +4

Why You See Eerily Accurate Ads

  • Hyper-Detailed Profiles: Tech companies track your age, location, web searches, clicks, and the things your friends and family search for. If you live with someone and they search for a product, you will likely see ads for it too.◉CBS News +1
  • The "Frequency Illusion": You see thousands of ads a day that mean nothing to you and forget them. You only notice the ones that happen to align with a recent conversation, creating the illusion of eavesdropping.KKaspersky
